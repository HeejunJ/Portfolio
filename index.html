SMART FACTORY AI
Smart Factory AI — 01

From Reactive to Predictive

Before
No predictive system — faults are only handled after they happen, causing serious delays.
MES / MCS track equipment status, but can't auto-detect cross-equipment anomalies.
A single AGV fault blocks its route until maintenance arrives — creating factory-wide traffic.
After — AI Module
Isolation Forest-based anomaly detection catches faults before they escalate.
The faulty AGV is auto-rerouted while a healthy robot picks up its job in real time.
RTD becomes the central hub — feeding MES, MCS & AHMS for one closed-loop response.
